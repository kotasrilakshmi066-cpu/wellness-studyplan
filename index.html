🏃 Detailed Exercise Guide

Cardio Exercises (45 min daily)

Boosts cellular respiration, burns fat, and improves blood microcirculation to the scalp hair follicles.

🚴 Jogging/Running (30 min) ~300 kcal

Begin with a 5-minute warm-up walk. Maintain a conversational pace (aerobic zone). Encourages follicle oxygenation.

🪢 Jump Rope Intervals (15 min) ~150 kcal

Intervals: 30 seconds high speed + 15 seconds rest. Repeat 10-15 cycles. Increases vascular elasticity.

⚡ High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) - 15 min alternative ~150 kcal

Perform burpees, mountain climbers, and high knees in 30s-on / 15s-off intervals. Excellent metabolic booster.

Strength Training (1h 30m, 3x/week)

Mon / Wed / Fri. Preserves lean body mass, boosts basal metabolic rate (BMR), and tones muscle.

Upper Body (30 min)

  • Push-ups: 3 sets × 10-15 reps
  • Dumbbell/Back Rows: 3 sets × 10 reps
  • Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 sets × 8-10 reps
  • Forearm Plank: 3 sets × 45-60 sec

Lower Body (30 min)

  • Goblet Squats: 3 sets × 15-20 reps
  • Alternating Lunges: 3 sets × 12 reps
  • Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets × 10 reps
  • Glute Bridges: 3 sets × 15 reps
🧠 Core & Stability: Planks, Russian twists, and superman holds. Maintains pelvic alignment and core stability.

Yoga for Hair Health (45 min daily)

Brings nutrient-rich blood supply directly to the scalp, reduces cortisol levels, and stabilizes hormones.

⬇️ Scalp Inversions (Hold 30+ seconds):
  • Downward Facing Dog: 5-10 breaths. Stretches spinal column and draws blood to face/head.
  • Standing Forward Fold (Uttanasana): 5-10 breaths. Maximizes circulation.
  • Supported Shoulder Stand (Sarvangasana): 30-60s. The queen of poses for thyroid and hormonal balance.
🌀 Spinal Twists & Heart Openers:
  • Seated Spinal Twist (Ardha Matsyendrasana): Stimulates liver/digestion and aids detoxification.
  • Camel Pose (Ustrasana): Opens thoracic region, regulates neck glands, and reduces stress.
  • Legs-Up-The-Wall (Viparita Karani): 5-10 minutes. Superb restorative pose for hormonal stabilization.

🌸 Hormone & Cycle Support Exercises

Pelvic floor relaxation and gentle movements help release blockages and promote menstrual regularity.

✅ Beneficial Poses: Butterfly Pose (Baddha Konasana) to open pelvic hips, Pigeon Pose, and Happy Baby.
⚠️ Exercises to Avoid: Extreme cardio or heavy resistance training exceeding 2 hours daily. Excessive training triggers hypothalamic amenorrhea (period disruption) due to energy deficiency.

✨ Scalp & Hair Core Routine

💆 Daily Scalp Stimulation

Physical traction activates cellular pathways to increase follicle thickness and diameter.

💆 Finger Scalp Massage (3 min)

Using clean, dry fingertips (not nails), apply moderate pressure and massage in circular patterns from the hairline, moving up to the crown, and down to the nape of the neck. Relieves micro-tension.

🌿 Oil massage (10 min)

Gently warm 1-2 drops of oil (Rosemary mixed with Jojoba/Coconut oil). Massage into the roots focusing on target areas. Perform daily or alternate days depending on hair type.

🤸 Inverted Hanging Position (3 min)

Sit on a chair or the edge of a bed, lower your torso, and let your head hang down between your knees. This gravity assist redirects cardiac output to the cerebral vascular network.

🧼 Weekly Cleansing & Conditioning

🚿 Wash Day (2-3x per week)

Use a sulfate-free, botanical shampoo. Focus shampoo application only on the scalp. Double wash if you have heavy oil accumulation. Rinse with lukewarm or cool water.

🧴 Deep Conditioning Treatment (1x per week)

Apply a repairing hair mask from mid-shaft to ends. Wrap hair in a warm micro-fiber towel and leave on for 15-20 minutes before rinsing. Locks in core moisture.

🥣 Natural Hair Nourishment Recipes

🧪 Rosemary Scalp Oil

Mix 5 drops of Rosemary Essential Oil with 2 tablespoons of Jojoba or Sweet Almond Oil. Rosemary has clinical efficacy comparable to 2% minoxidil in stimulating hair follicles.

🍃 Soothing Aloe Vera Mask

Apply fresh organic aloe vera gel directly to the scalp 30 minutes before shampooing. Calms scalp inflammation, regulates sebum production, and clears dandruff.
